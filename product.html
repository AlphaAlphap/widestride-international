Investment universe

What the mandate may hold

Low-risk sleeve

  • Federal Government Treasury Bills issued by the Central Bank of Nigeria
  • Investment-grade corporate bonds, rated by SEC-registered agencies such as Agusto & Co.
  • Money market funds
  • Fixed and term deposit investments

Medium-risk sleeve

  • Quoted dividend-paying equities of established companies
  • OTC-registered unquoted equities
  • Actively traded, SEC-approved stock indices
  • Publicly listed REITs

Fees

Clear costs, with a hard ceiling.

Management fees are 2.75% of AUM per annum, accruing daily and payable quarterly. That figure includes 0.25% regulatory fees for discretionary business, remitted to the Commission.

Clients also bear custody fees of 0.05%, bank charges, and applicable taxes. Direct and indirect fees together will not exceed 3.5% of AUM annually.

Emergency liquidation carries a 1% fee on the asset, plus any market-impact costs. Outstanding obligations are cleared before funds are released.

Dividends

Income, paid in step with the book.

Quarterly dividends are proportional to each investor’s holding. Investors may elect a Dividend Reinvestment Plan (DRIP). Withholding tax is deducted where applicable.

Low-risk sleeve — estimated yield reference of 20% per annum from interest income on government and corporate bonds and money-market instruments.

Medium-risk sleeve — estimated yield reference of 30% per annum from dividend-paying equities and listed REITs.

If the product experiences low returns or losses, distributions may be reduced or suspended. A contingency reserve is maintained for fund health.

Liquidity

A contingency sleeve, and an emergency path.

  • 20% of fixed-income securities are held in interest-yielding call or money-market balances as contingency funds.
  • Emergency requests may be considered for medical need, sudden income loss, legal obligation, or other exceptional cases.
  • Partial liquidation within the contingency threshold is reviewed and processed within 24 hours.
  • Full liquidation, or partial liquidation above that threshold, is reviewed and, if approved, disbursed within 24 hours of receipt.
