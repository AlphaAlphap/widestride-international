Important notices

Please read these notes before relying on anything on this website. They sit alongside, and do not replace, the product paper and any executed mandate.

Private product

WIL Balanced Asset is a discretionary private product of Widestride International Limited (RC 6973596). This website is an introduction. It is not a prospectus, not a public offer of securities, and not an invitation to the public to subscribe.

No advice

Nothing on this site is investment, tax, or legal advice. Suitability depends on your circumstances, objectives, and risk tolerance. You should obtain independent advice and read the product paper, custody terms, and fee schedule before appointing the firm.

Performance figures

References to 25% per annum, 20% and 30% historical or estimated yields, and similar figures are taken from the firm’s product paper. They are illustrations and references, not guarantees. Markets, credit, liquidity, fees, and tax will affect actual results. Capital is at risk.

Fees and tax

Management fees, custody charges, bank charges, emergency liquidation fees, and applicable taxes are as described in the product paper. Dividends are subject to applicable tax laws; withholding tax may be deducted.

Custody

Client securities under the product are intended to be held with the nominated custodian, Rand Merchant Bank Ltd, which maintains accounts, collects benefits, and manages holdings as described in the product paper.

Emergency liquidation

Early or emergency liquidation is exceptional, may be full or partial, is subject to review, and may incur a 1% fee plus market-impact costs. It is not a substitute for the 180-day minimum initial investment period.
