The house

A private product, held with institutional care.

Widestride International Limited manages WIL Balanced Asset as a discretionary private product. The portfolio is constructed to combine growth potential with risk mitigation, in a manner that aligns with each investor’s objectives and risk profile.

Capital is allocated between low-risk instruments — treasury bills, investment-grade bonds, money-market funds, and term deposits — and medium-risk holdings in SEC-approved equities and publicly listed REITs.

Client securities are held with a nominated custodian, Rand Merchant Bank Ltd. Reporting is monthly, with quarterly reviews and an annual statement.

Custody & conduct

Securities held apart. Rules kept close.

  • Custodian: Rand Merchant Bank Ltd — accounts, benefits, and holdings.
  • Investments primarily in Nigerian assets registered by SEC Nigeria.
  • No more than 10% of the portfolio in any single security.
  • Total fees capped at 3.5% of AUM annually, including management and custody.
  • Emergency liquidity window, with 20% of the fixed-income sleeve held as contingency.
