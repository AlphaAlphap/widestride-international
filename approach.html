Construction & control

How the book is built and kept.

The portfolio balances risk and return through a defined mix of asset classes, SEC-aligned guidelines, independent custody, and a published monitoring cadence.

Guidelines

01

SEC first

The portfolio adheres to SEC regulations and guidelines at all times. Investments are primarily in Nigerian assets duly registered by the Securities and Exchange Commission.

02

Concentration cap

Exposure to any single security will not exceed 10% of total portfolio value, so that one name cannot dominate the mandate.

03

Grade and approval

Fixed-income holdings are investment-grade, with ratings from SEC-registered agencies. Equity and REIT exposure is limited to approved and registered instruments.

Custodian

Rand Merchant Bank Ltd.

A custodian is nominated under WIL Balanced Asset. Rand Merchant Bank Ltd is responsible for:

  • Maintaining the accounts of clients’ securities
  • Collecting the benefits and rights accruing to clients
  • Managing the holding of clients in respect of securities held under the product

Income and redemption proceeds are paid directly to the custody account.

Reporting

You should never have to guess the book.

Monthly

Performance reports covering portfolio composition, returns, and market commentary.

Quarterly

Valuation of the portfolio, the basis of that valuation, alignment with client objectives, and rebalancing as needed.

Annually

A statement of overall performance for the fiscal year, together with expenses and costs incurred.

Risk

Principal risks, and how they are met.

RiskMitigating factors
Market risk The portfolio team tracks interest rates and inflation; matches asset and liability maturities; uses Commission-approved hedging where appropriate; and maintains an allocation suited to each investor’s risk tolerance.
Regulatory risk A dedicated compliance team headed by a SEC-certified professional; ongoing monitoring of product activity and regulatory change; reporting to the Commission and other applicable Nigerian authorities.
Liquidity risk A balance between liquid and less-liquid assets, kept dynamic and informed by monthly liquidity projections, with a dedicated contingency sleeve.
Credit risk Diversification; restriction to SEC-approved and/or investment-grade assets; ongoing review of issuer health through financial reports, rating agencies, and economic events.
